Questions from the helicopter.

Where do I use my own logs?

In the downloaded HTML file, opened locally. Its script and styles are embedded, network connections are blocked, and it saves no session. This page only demonstrates invented text. Your browser, extensions and device still need to be trusted.

Will it catch everything?

No. It recognises common credentials, emails, wallet addresses, key blocks, card numbers, IPv4 addresses and home-folder usernames. Names, addresses and private sentences can pass through unchanged. Add exact phrases, read the entire preview and remove anything else before export. It cannot undo a disclosure or stop someone with access to your agent.

When does the relay take off?

Coming soon. We are building toward direct shielded ZEC payments and a hosted agent relay. For now, take the free redactor for a spin; passes will open when the landing pad is ready.

Do you track me?

This site loads no analytics, no third-party scripts and no web fonts. Our hosting provider still sees ordinary request logs, like every website.
