memory-cleaner.py LOCAL CLI

First supported store: LangGraph SQLite. Python 3.10+. No packages to install, no uploads, no wallet access. Try an invented demo first:

python3 memory-cleaner.py demo --db demo.sqlite
python3 memory-cleaner.py audit --db demo.sqlite --plan audit.json
python3 memory-cleaner.py clean --db demo.sqlite --plan audit.json --thread thread-0001

The last command previews removal. Applying needs a separate confirmation and a stopped agent. Cleanup removes the selected thread's entire saved history and pending writes; other threads stay intact.

Matches are possible payment traces, including challenges and failed attempts. They do not prove a payment happened. Local cleanup cannot erase blockchain, provider or backup records.

Questions from the helicopter.

What can I scan?

The first supported store is a local LangGraph SQLite checkpoint database, using the SqliteSaver 3.1.1 layout. The Python 3.10+ script checks saved checkpoints and pending writes for possible x402 payment markers. Follow the guide and stop your agent before applying cleanup.

Does it remove just the receipt?

It removes the entire saved history and pending writes of each thread you explicitly select. Preview that selection before applying it. Other threads stay intact. It does not edit individual messages or erase blockchain, provider or backup records.

Will it find every payment?

No. Matches can be challenges or failed attempts rather than settled payments. A scan with no matches does not prove there are no payment records; other stores and unrecognised formats are outside its scope.

Is z402 protecting my payments already?

z402 is at the design stage. The cleaner discovers and removes supported local records; automatic protection during payment needs the separate proof, settlement and transport implementation.

Do you track me?

The cleaner runs on your machine with no uploads or telemetry. This site loads no analytics, third-party scripts or web fonts. Our hosting provider sees ordinary website request logs.
